Career Advancement System — Feedback

1. How many hours did you play?

9 hours (I'm still playing)

2. For which faction?

Confederation

3. What did you like?

I liked that the Career Advancement System shows the player what they will receive in the future, including a reward, which will motivate them to play more. It's also a very excellent reward.

Buildings now unlock automatically once the required number of stars is reached. This makes the game much more enjoyable than waiting days for a new building to unlock, which could cause a player to abandon the game for two consecutive days — a bad sign. However, thanks to the Career Advancement System, this problem has been solved.

4. What didn't you like?

The Career Advancement System contains a major problem that will render the campaign pointless. I will explain the problem from the perspective of a new player and an old player.

New player

If a new player notices that a Career Advancement System grants new troops, heroes, game modes, and buildings, while the campaign offers nothing and has a meager reward, they might ignore it, especially since the campaign missions are ordinary, uncomplicated, and uninteresting.

Old player

Let's talk about how players are reacting to the campaign, then we'll discuss the problem of the Career Advancement System.

If you look at player profiles in the game, you'll notice that they haven't completed a 3-star campaign or one on high difficulty. This is because the campaign is unexciting and only grants new troops and buildings if a player earns one star on each mission within the campaign. In short, they are ignoring the campaign because it is unexciting and too simple — a campaign that does not fulfill its essential role.

Now let's talk about the problem of the Career Advancement System with veteran players. Veteran players will immediately realize that the campaign is no longer useful because the Career Advancement System only grants everything by collecting stars. Players will simply ignore the campaign.

Camping players

This type of player will ignore a live campaign because they will prefer not to take any stars from the campaign and prefer that this number of stars continue to play 1v1 battles. They will get more money and complete contracts, ignoring the campaign. This is very annoying.

In the Career Advancement System, you'll notice that Viking, Delta, Vertex, and other forces have a tank emblem next to them. There should also be an aircraft emblem for Vertex and a ship emblem for Viking.

The Rank 30 medal should be changed to another type of medal because a player needs 10 years to reach this rank, which is extremely difficult; he deserves something better.

The missions in the campaign are very simple, especially the first mission in the campaign.

There was also a problem I encountered with Viking before the mission that was explained, so I was able to use Viking in missions where it wasn't present before the Career Advancement System, and because of this I was able to win easily.

5. What can be done better in your opinion?

1. Redesign the first mission

The first mission in the campaign needs to be completely redesigned. I suggest adding heroes, planes, a range of modifications, adding a larger map, and completely changing the dialogue.

2. Increase the difficulty of the remaining missions

The remaining campaign missions need to be tweaked; I mean, they should be a little more difficult. The player should feel the challenge. If you replay all the campaign missions, you'll notice that you can always win easily; there's no difficulty at all.

A solution must be found for players who will ignore an upcoming campaign, such as the requirement to complete a certain number of campaign tasks to advance to the next rank.

Suggestion: Skills and the Career Advancement System

I have a suggestion:

The addition of a Career Advancement System should be used to grant each rank or league a specific number of allowed skills. There is a solution for players who already have 200 skills at Rank 9.

For players who already have 200 skills at Rank 9 or any other rank, those skills will be deactivated and their money will be refunded.

Second solution: those skills will remain available but will be inactive and have no effect.

This will solve many problems and make battles more balanced because I might face an enemy at Rank 9 who has more than 250 skills, which is illogical; he should only have 60 or 70, not 250.

Utilizing a Career Advancement System in this matter, such as adding a specific number of skills to each rank or league within the Career Advancement System, such as 300 skills for the Diamond League.

Why should I face an enemy whose account is Rank 9, whose account is 5 years old, and who has 250 skills? This will drive new players away from the game.
